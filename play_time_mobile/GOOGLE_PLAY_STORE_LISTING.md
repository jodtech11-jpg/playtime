## Full description

```
Play Time is your mobile home for sports—find venues, book courts, meet players, and shop gear. Built for players and busy weekends across Tamil Nadu and India.

BOOK COURTS & SLOTS
Browse nearby turfs and courts with maps, search, and filters by sport, price, distance, rating, and amenities. Book full courts or shared slots for badminton, cricket, football, and more. Pay securely and get a booking pass with QR code for check-in.

TEAM-UP
Split the court and the cost. Join open team-up slots or create your own so others can fill empty spots—perfect for badminton singles and doubles.

MEMBERSHIPS
Buy venue memberships for better rates and benefits. Track status in the app; venues activate plans when ready.

SOCIAL FEED
Share match results and highlights at your favourite venues. Comment, repost, and keep the rivalry friendly.

MARKETPLACE
Browse sports products, cart checkout, and track orders from your profile.

STAY IN THE LOOP
Get push notifications for bookings, team-ups, memberships, tournaments, flash deals, and more. Manage preferences in settings.

SIGN IN YOUR WAY
Log in with your Indian mobile number (OTP) or Google—the choice is yours.

Whether you’re booking after work or planning a weekend match, Play Time keeps discovery, booking, and community in one app. Download and find your next game.
```
